          The reasoning layer never writes to the transit ledger — an inspection adjudicates a
          parcel's condition, it does not revise what origin recorded. Its output goes here
          instead, one appended line per decision, carrying the ledger digest it was reconciled
          against. Served from GET /api/v1/detections and
          GET /api/v1/exceptions, backed by SQLite at
          logs/engine.db.

The five answers this API can return

        three judge the parcel · two are the router declining to
      

      
Verdicts on the parcel itself

      

        

          EXCEPTION_FLAGGED
          
Damage seen above the confidence threshold, reconciled against the ledger.

        

        

          CLEAR
          
A parcel was located confidently and no damage was found. A negative you can trust.

        

        

          INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION
          
Evidence too weak, or the frame unreadable. The language model is never called here.

        

      


      
Router outcomes — the detector was not the right tool

      

        

          UNSUPPORTED_CAPABILITY
          
The question is about something this model was never trained to see, such as people.

        

        

          ANSWERED_WITHOUT_VISION
          
Answered from the transit record alone. No inference was spent.

        

      

      

        Honest limits. The detector reaches 0.625 mAP50 on damage over a
        held-out test split, but per-source recall still ranges from 0.000 to 0.934, so accuracy
        depends on how closely an image resembles the training capture styles. That is exactly
        why the guardrail exists: a detector this uneven must refuse rather than narrate. Full
        evidence, including five real failure cases, is in MEMO.md.
