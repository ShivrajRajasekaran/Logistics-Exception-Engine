Evaluation graphs

Confusion matrix, precision–recall curve and training curves for the shipped v3 checkpoint on the 939-image held-out test split. These are the exact committed images behind the numbers quoted in MEMO.md.

Confusion matrix

Normalised. Rows are true class, columns are predicted class. The background row/column captures missed detections and phantom boxes on empty space.

Precision–recall curve

Per class, across the confidence sweep used for mAP. Shows the trade-off this project's 0.65 guardrail threshold is chosen against.

Training curves

Loss and mAP across all completed epochs of the v3 run. Best checkpoint was selected by Ultralytics fitness, not simply the last epoch.
